How it works

Three layers, one camera.

A learned policy does the broad motion, a classical servo does the last centimetres, and a proprioceptive primitive decides when to let go.

01

Frozen policy

Reads 8 grayscale wrist frames (280 ms) plus 12 joint values and predicts an 8×6 action chunk at 25 Hz.

02

Visual servo

Back-projects the part and tote onto the known work plane, leads the moving part and gates the close at predicted contact.

03

Release primitive

Confirms a stalled close, a lift and a low descent before opening the jaws, so “touched the tote” never counts as a place.

The wobble

It worked, but it shook. Here is why.

The policy emits one arm target every 40 ms. The simulator held each target constant and then jumped to the next one, so the position servo followed a staircase at 25 Hz. Interpolating inside each step removes the staircase without touching the policy.

Results

Fresh seeds, frozen checkpoint.

200 trials per condition. A place means the part was released, stayed in the tote and moved slower than 0.05 m/s for 0.4 s. The evaluation objects have zero overlap with the 2,302 training seeds.

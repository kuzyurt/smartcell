# Asset provenance

Every asset in the cell either comes from a public, licensed source or is
built in this repository from primitives.

## Robot: SO-101 (5 DoF + gripper)

- Source: `google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie`, `so101/so101.xml`
- Licence: Apache-2.0
- Not copied into this tree. `build_cell._attach` loads it from the installed
  menagerie package. The cell adds a `grasp` site inside the `gripper` body and
  changes material colours; the dynamics are as published.

## Conveyor: MuJoCo `surfacevel` carousel

- Source: `google-deepmind/mujoco`, `model/surfacevel/carousel.xml`
  (feature commit `4787c8094c13e95bbd7423c0f6607f0cea0b0261`, "geom surfacevel")
- Licence: Apache-2.0
- Unmodified upstream copy: `assets/_upstream_mujoco_carousel.xml`
- Adapted file used by the scene: `assets/conveyor.xml`

The upstream belt is 0.60 m wide. The SO-101 reaches about 0.35 m, and the
only lane it can reach is 0.245 m from its base, so the belt was narrowed and
the guides moved:

| Upstream | Here | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| belt `0.74 0.30 0.06` | `0.715 0.048 0.025` | lane within reach; belt ends tangent to the rollers so a part rides over the crown and drops off |
| rails at `y = ±0.33`, one 1.48 m box per side | `y = ±0.07`, two segments per side with an opening at `x = -0.02 … +0.22` | 140 mm lane for the fingers; the wrist needs the opening to turn top-down over the belt |
| rollers `r 0.06` | `r 0.025` | roller crown flush with the belt surface |
| `surfacevel 0.7` | `0.10 m/s` | the part stays in the 160 mm grasp window for a full pick |
| roller `surfacevel -11.667` | `-4.0` | `v / r` for 0.10 m/s and `r = 0.025` |

The belt was later lengthened by 200 mm at the tail end only (tail roller from
`x = -0.74` to `x = -0.94`), so the pick window and discharge point did not
move. The belt has no joints or actuators; `nu = 6` is the arm alone.

The frame around the belt (extruded side beams, legs, feet, guards, gear
motor, control cabinet, photo-eye) is original work in `line_frame.py`. Apart
from the flare guides that centre the part, all of it is visual only.

## Table: furniture_sim simple table

- Source: `vikashplus/furniture_sim`, `simpleTable.xml`
- Licence: Apache-2.0, text in `assets/LICENSE_furniture_sim.txt`
- Unmodified upstream copy: `assets/_upstream_simpleTable.xml`
- Adapted file used by the scene: `assets/table.xml`

In the upstream file the top floats slightly above the apron and the legs pass
through it. `table.xml` keeps the dimensions (1.20 × 0.75 × 0.625 m) and moves
the parts so they touch.

## Hall

No openly licensed MJCF factory hall was available, so `factory_hall.py`
builds one from boxes: floor markings, walls, columns, a roller door, roof
beams and deck, lamp fixtures and a few pallets and crates. It is original
work.

## Parts

The parts are 32 mm cubes. The plate that carries the fixed finger sits
7.5 mm below the grasp site, so larger parts collide with it: in tests, 30 mm
and 34 mm parts were lifted, 38 mm and larger were pushed off the belt.

## MuJoCo 3.14 renderer notes

Things that behaved differently from what the documentation suggests, measured
while building the scene:

- At most 8 lights are rendered; the cell uses exactly 8.
- Directional lights and spot lights pointing upwards had no visible effect.
- `light_intensity` is ignored; brightness comes from `diffuse`.
- A model built with `MjSpec` has an all-zero headlight; `scene_look.polish`
  sets it explicitly.
- Material `specular` and `shininess` had no effect in the offscreen
  `Renderer`.
- Changing `mat_rgba` on a compiled `MjModel` does not reach the renderer;
  look changes have to go through the spec.
